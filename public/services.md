# Services — Innovators AI HUB

Innovators AI HUB rents AI co-worker agents and produces AI brand films. An agent is scoped to one process, measured by one number, and handed over with a named point where a person takes over.

Canonical page: https://www.innovatorsaihub.com/services
Pricing: https://www.innovatorsaihub.com/pricing.md

## The four agent services

1. **WhatsApp AI agent** — inbound enquiries, qualification, booking, support. https://www.innovatorsaihub.com/whatsapp-ai-agent
2. **LinkedIn AI agent** — prospecting, personalised outreach drafted for human approval, follow-up. https://www.innovatorsaihub.com/linkedin-ai-agent
3. **Voice calling AI agent** — outbound and inbound calls, qualification, appointment setting. https://www.innovatorsaihub.com/voice-ai-agent-use-cases
4. **Custom AI workflows** — multi-agent operational setups, CRM integrations, logging, hand-off rules and reporting.

## AI brand films

Cinematic shorts built with generative video, blending live action and synthetic environments, for launches, investor decks, landing pages and paid social. Custom pricing.

## How an engagement runs

Audit the one process, configure the agent, test on real conversations, go live, review weekly. Setup and the CRM connection are part of the plan; channel usage charges from Meta or telephony providers are separate.
