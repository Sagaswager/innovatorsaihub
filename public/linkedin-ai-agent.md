# LinkedIn AI Agent — Innovators AI HUB

A LinkedIn AI agent does the outbound prospecting work around a salesperson rather than instead of them: it builds and refines the target list, drafts personalised opening messages from the profile, schedules follow-ups, and stops the moment someone replies. The person whose name is on the message approves and sends it.

Canonical page: https://www.innovatorsaihub.com/linkedin-ai-agent
Limits and policy: https://www.innovatorsaihub.com/linkedin-automation-limits

## What it does

- Target list building from an ideal customer profile: role, seniority, industry, geography, company size
- Personalised opening messages written from something real on the profile
- Follow-up on a schedule, with stop conditions on reply, booking or opt-out
- Reply detection and hand-off: qualified conversations become booked calls with context
- Network housekeeping: sorting requests and inbound DMs
- Reporting: conversations started, replies, meetings booked, which message earned the reply

## What it does not do

It does not log into LinkedIn and send on your behalf. LinkedIn's User Agreement prohibits bots and unauthorised automated methods for adding contacts or sending messages, so the send stays with a person, volume stays human-scale, and sending is spread across working hours.
