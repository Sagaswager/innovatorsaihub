# WhatsApp AI Agent — Innovators AI HUB

A WhatsApp AI agent reads and answers messages on your business number: it qualifies enquiries, books appointments or meetings, answers repeated questions and writes the outcome into your CRM. It answers at any hour, and hands the conversation to a person when the rules you set say so.

## What it does

- Replies to inbound enquiries in seconds, including outside working hours
- Qualifies by asking the five or so questions your sales process actually uses
- Books meetings or appointments into a calendar the team already keeps
- Answers repeat questions — timings, location, fees, preparation, order status
- Follows up on conversations that went quiet, on a schedule
- Writes source, intent, status and outcome into the CRM you already use

## Where a person takes over

Pricing exceptions, refunds, complaints, clinical or legal questions, and anyone who asks for a human. The hand-off carries the transcript and the qualification fields, not just "please call".

## Prerequisites on your side

A business number eligible for the WhatsApp Business API, a verified business profile on Meta, and access to the calendar or CRM the agent writes into. Meta approves the number, not us, and that approval sits on Meta's timeline.
